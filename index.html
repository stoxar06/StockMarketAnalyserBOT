How it works

Built and validated in five phases — no phase is skipped, and no real order is placed until paper trading proves the system.

Data→ Strategy→ Backtest→ Paper trade→ Live (small)
1🔐

Setup & secure auth

Connect to Zerodha Kite Connect. API keys live in a local .env file — never shared, never committed. You log in daily; the bot never holds your password.

2📈

Data

Pull historical candles into a local database and stream live prices over WebSocket — building its own candles in real time for the strategy to read.

3🧮

Strategy & honest backtest

One simple, fully-specified rule (a moving-average crossover to start), backtested over real history with brokerage, taxes, and slippage subtracted.

4📝

Paper trade

Run live during market hours in DRY_RUN mode — logging simulated trades instead of placing real orders — until results match the backtest.

5🚦

Live (small) with controls

Only after paper trading passes, and only with stop-loss, position sizing, and a kill switch — starting tiny, scaling slowly.

★🧷

Safety everywhere

Equity cash only (no leverage), real broker-state reconciliation on startup, and graceful handling of partial fills, rejections, and outages.

Risk controls (non-negotiable)

Every live strategy must include these — the difference between learning and blowing up.

✓

Per-trade stop-loss

A hard, automatic exit on every position (with the honest caveat that gaps and circuits can slip past it).

✓

Position sizing ≤ 1%

Risk at most ~1% of capital on any single trade, with guards against over-sizing.

✓

Daily-loss kill switch

If the day's loss limit is hit, the bot halts for the day — and the state survives a restart.

✓

DRY_RUN by default

Live order placement is off until explicitly, manually enabled by the owner.
